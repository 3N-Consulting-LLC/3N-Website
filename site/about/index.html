About 3N Consulting

We’re a Maryland-based management consulting firm. We help public agencies, educational institutions, and businesses make difficult decisions and carry them through.

Our story

Nick Gupta founded 3N Consulting in 2023 after returning to Maryland. He wanted to apply two decades of consulting experience to the challenges facing organizations in the region.

By 2024, 3N’s core team was in place. The firm has since supported government agencies, colleges, and businesses on work ranging from centralized purchasing to workforce planning and finance operations.
