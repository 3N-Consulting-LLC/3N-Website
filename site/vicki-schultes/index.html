About Vicki

Expertise

  • Financial Management & CFO Services

Victoria L. Schultes (“Vicki”) is an accomplished public-sector professional with nearly 30 years of government experience, including more than 25 years specializing in regulatory affairs, legislative processes, and administrative operations.

Her expertise encompasses regulatory and policy development, statutory implementation, government operations, process improvement, and executive advisory services. Throughout her career, Vicki has collaborated with executive leadership, legal counsel, legislators, and subject matter experts to translate complex statutory and policy requirements into practical, sustainable solutions.

Vicki has led initiatives to modernize regulatory review processes, strengthen organizational accountability, improve policy management, and establish structured approaches to statutory compliance. Her experience also includes contributing to the development of electronic regulatory management systems and facilitating cross-functional collaboration.

She brings a thoughtful, solutions-oriented approach to helping government organizations improve operational effectiveness, regulatory consistency, and administrative efficiency.
