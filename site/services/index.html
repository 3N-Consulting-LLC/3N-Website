Organizational Diagnostics

We start with O-RAM, our Organizational Readiness Assessment Methodology. It identifies what is holding the organization back and where to focus improvement. Our founder developed O-RAM while leading organizational assessment and redesign at a global consulting firm. It has since been used across companies, public agencies, and colleges.

What you get

  • Current-state organizational assessment
  • Stakeholder alignment map
  • Capacity and capability analysis
  • Risk and readiness scoring
  • Prioritized improvement roadmap

Six dimensions of organizational readiness

O-RAM scores each dimension and combines them into an overall assessment.

Leadership

Is there clear sponsorship, direction, and accountability for the change?

Structure

Do roles, reporting lines, and spans of control fit the work?

Culture & Communication

Do people understand the change, trust it, and talk about it openly?

Technology & Systems

Do the tools and data support the way work needs to get done?

Governance & Processes

Are decisions, policies, and workflows defined, followed, and compliant?

Metrics & Rewards

Do measures and incentives support the outcomes you want?
