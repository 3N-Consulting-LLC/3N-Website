Who we serve State agencies and commissions, local governments, education systems, and workforce development organizations

All service lines

Challenges we solve

Plans with no owners

Priorities lack clear owners, budgets, or timelines.

Competing stakeholder priorities

Different interests make consensus difficult.

Limited visibility into results

Leaders lack the measures they need to assess performance.

Fragmented data

Reporting across systems is manual, late, or inconsistent.

What we offer

Economic development strategy

Assess regional innovation ecosystems and benchmark peer programs to inform economic development priorities.

Strategic and statewide plans

Develop multi-year plans with clear goals, strategies, owners, and measures.

Stakeholder engagement and facilitation

Use surveys, focus groups, listening sessions, and working groups to agree on priorities.

KPIs and performance management

Dashboards and data visualization

Program evaluation

Benchmarking

Compare performance with peers to set defensible targets.
