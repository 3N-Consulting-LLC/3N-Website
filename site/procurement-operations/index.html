Procurement & Operations Transformation

We assess where you stand, quantify savings opportunities, and put policies, processes, and tools in place to improve purchasing and operations.

Who we serve State agencies, local governments, colleges and universities, and mid-market companies

All service lines

Challenges we solve

Decentralized buying

Independent purchasing reduces buying power and makes it harder to see what the organization spends.

Unclear savings

Inconsistent spend analysis leaves savings opportunities unquantified.

Audit and compliance risk

Outdated policies and uneven enforcement can lead to audit findings.

Slow, manual processes

Email and paper slow down requisitions, approvals, and contracts.

What we offer

Procurement maturity assessment

Assess your procurement function against a maturity model and identify the gaps to address first.

Spend analysis and savings strategy

Reconcile spend across departments and vendors and quantify savings by category and scenario.

Centralization and operating model

Design the structure, roles, and governance for centralized or hybrid procurement.

Policy and process redesign

Update policies, approval workflows, and contract tools to reduce risk and cycle times.

Procurement technology adoption

Define requirements and support the rollout of e-procurement and contract management tools.

Lean operations improvement

Remove unnecessary steps so teams can handle more work with existing staff.

How we deliver

Organizational Diagnostics

Assess procurement maturity and organizational readiness with O-RAM.

Data & Decision Analytics

Analyze spend, model savings, and benchmark vendors.

Enterprise Solutions

Implement procurement policies, contracts, and purchasing tools, and train the staff who will use them.
